Title: Vas
Place: Barcelona, Spain, 2025
Tags: 
Location: Barcelona, Spain

VAS is a pair of vases that explores the act of containing as a gesture to protect and to hold. They reinterpret Mediterranean archetypes through a contemporary lens, where function and meaning coexist. The dialogue between a warm, porous exterior made from recycled cork and a precise 3D-printed ceramic interior, with its process left visible, transforms waste into value and imperfection into language.

Their dual structure creates a dialogue allowing technology to become part between earth and craft, softness and of the object’s expression. The pieces precision.
